# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here are quick answers to common questions about shopping, payment, and managing your Northstar Retail Co. orders.

### Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
Because orders move quickly to our packing team, shipping addresses can only be updated within **30 minutes** of placing your order. Once an order status switches to "Processing" or "Shipped", we cannot alter the address. Please double-check your address at checkout.

### Can I combine two separate orders into one package?
Currently, our automated warehouse system processes each order number independently to ensure speed and tracking accuracy. As a result, we cannot combine separate orders into a single shipment.
