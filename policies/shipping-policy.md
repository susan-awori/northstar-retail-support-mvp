# Northstar Retail Co. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Northstar Retail Co. partners with trusted international and regional couriers to ensure fast, reliable delivery of your orders directly to your doorstep.

## Shipping Options & Delivery Timeframes
We offer standard and express shipping tiers depending on your location:
- **Standard Shipping (3–5 Business Days)**: Free on all orders over $75. Orders under $75 incur a flat $4.99 shipping fee.
- **Express Courier Shipping (1–2 Business Days)**: Available at checkout for a flat $14.99 fee across major metro areas.
