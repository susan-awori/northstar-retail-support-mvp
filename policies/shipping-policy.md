# Northstar Retail Co. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Northstar Retail Co. partners with trusted international and regional couriers to ensure fast, reliable delivery of your orders directly to your doorstep.

## Shipping Options & Delivery Timeframes
We offer standard and express shipping tiers depending on your location:
- **Standard Shipping (3–5 Business Days)**: Free on all orders over $75. Orders under $75 incur a flat $4.99 shipping fee.
- **Express Courier Shipping (1–2 Business Days)**: Available at checkout for a flat $14.99 fee across major metro areas.
Orders placed before 2:00 PM local time are processed and packed on the same business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on weekends ship on the following business day.

## Carrier Partners & Real-Time Tracking
Orders are dispatched via FedEx Express, DHL Express, Kenya Post Logistics, or Speedaf Express depending on your region. Once your order leaves our fulfillment center, you will receive an automated email containing a 6-digit tracking link and carrier tracking ID

## Delays & Hold Handling
While we strive for prompt delivery, severe weather, customs inspections, or holiday peaks may cause unexpected delays. If your order status reads **On Hold** or **Delayed**, our logistics team is actively coordinating with the carrier. Order tracking statuses refresh automatically every 24 hours.

## International Shipping
We currently ship to over 40 countries worldwide. Customs duties, taxes, and import tariffs are calculated at checkout where applicable. International deliveries typically take 7–12 business days depending on destination customs clearance.
